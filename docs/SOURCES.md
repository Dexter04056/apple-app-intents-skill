## Evidence cautions

- WWDC demonstrations explain intended use and show sample behavior. A current SDK symbol page and generated Xcode template control exact spellings, types, optionality, and availability.
- Apple Newsroom and keynote material establish rollout statements as of their dates. They do not establish device-level success for a third-party app.
- Cassinelli's WWDC26 articles often annotate Apple demonstrations. Benchmark numbers and API behavior should be attributed to Apple unless independently measured.
- No third-party article, playlist, package compilation, or AppIntentsTesting result substitutes for a manual Siri workflow on the intended runtime configuration.
- Full third-party transcripts and articles are not reproduced in this repository. The links above remain under their publishers' terms.
